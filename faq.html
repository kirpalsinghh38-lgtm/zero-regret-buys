Why don’t you show prices?

Because they’d be wrong by tomorrow. Amazon changes prices constantly, and typing a number here would mislead you. Click through — you’ll see today’s live price before you decide.

Do you earn a commission?

Yes. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Every product link on this site is a paid link, labeled as one — the commission costs you nothing extra.

Are these medical recommendations?

No. These are general wellness descriptions, not medical advice. Supplements interact with medications and conditions — talk to your pharmacist or clinician before starting anything new.

Why only thirty products?

One product per reel episode. The list grows as new episodes drop — that’s the whole format. A hundred-item catalog would just be another store.
