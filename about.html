HOW WE PICK

Four rules. Miss one, and it never makes the page.

  1. One clear job. Every product gets a single “Best for” — if we can’t name the job in three words, it doesn’t belong here.
  2. Label math you can verify. The right form (glycinate, not oxide; citrate, not oxide), the dose stated clearly, and a facts panel that checks out. You can check every claim on the actual label — that’s the point.
  3. No invented ratings. No stars, no scores, no “9.7/10”. The buying criteria are the review.
  4. No typed prices, ever. Prices move daily; Amazon shows the live one when you click. We’d rather be accurate than impressive.

We don’t accept payment for placement. Brands can’t buy a card — the affiliate commission is the entire business model, and it’s disclosed everywhere.
